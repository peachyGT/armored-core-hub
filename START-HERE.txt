Armored Core Hub

Open ArmoredCoreHub.exe, choose Add games and select your game folder.
The selected game's setup checklist shows what still needs to be added.
The Hub downloads and sets up the required emulator for you.

Windows 10 or 11, x64. No separate .NET installation is needed.
Supply your own legally obtained game files and required BIOS dumps.

Portable ZIP: extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder before starting.
Keep the files together. Your library, settings and saves live beside the app.
Back up that folder before replacing its application files with a new version.

Installer: click Install, then open the Hub from its Start menu shortcut.
Reinstalling an update preserves your library, settings and saves. Uninstalling
removes installed app files and shortcuts; it leaves your personal Hub data.
An existing portable copy remains separate from the installed copy.

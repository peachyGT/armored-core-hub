# Compatibility and current limits

The Hub is still in development. Our testing covers sampled launches and gameplay on our test machines, rather than full playthroughs or every console/region edition. Xbox 360 checks have been limited to Armored Core V and Verdict Day; the detailed frame-time measurements cover V. Earlier PlayStation, PS2, PSP and PS3 sessions sampled a selection of editions.

The catalog contains 15 games, 19 console/Portable library entries and 100 regional/product/disc records. It does not include Armored Core VI or mobile releases. Product-ID recognition identifies an edition; it does not establish that a dump matches a verified reference hash.

- Unreadable, encrypted or nonstandard media may require a different supported dump format. The Hub does not decrypt PS3 discs. Supported decrypted PS3 ISO/Joliet media can be prepared for the recommended RPCS3 build while retaining the original.
- AC4/For Answer PS3 recommendations include an older RPCS3 fallback sampled after first-mission freezes on a newer build. An upstream Playable rating does not guarantee every mission on this PC.
- ACV on Xenia was sampled with graphics repairs and a higher-frame-rate configuration. Cutscenes, AI, all missions and long-term timing have not been fully verified. An FPS limit alone does not unlock a game.
- Default keyboard mappings and selected physical-controller paths have been exercised. Every Community layout, device and hot-reconnect combination has not been checked in gameplay.
- Achievement tracking is available only where the provider/game supplies it. Five early title/platform combinations had published RetroAchievements sets at the last check. PS3 AC4 and For Answer have no native trophies. Region and patched-copy support depends on provider hashes. Live RetroAchievements authentication and actual unlocks remain unverified.
- Artwork, video and transparent-logo extraction depend on usable game assets and supported formats. A manual artwork option and still/cover fallbacks remain available.
- Arbitrary save conversion, network multiplayer setup, commercial title-update acquisition and native mouse aiming are not included.

If you encounter a problem, use the [bug-report form](https://github.com/peachyGT/armored-core-hub/issues/new?template=bug.yml) and include the game, platform, region, Hub/emulator version and steps. Keep account credentials and game files out of public reports.

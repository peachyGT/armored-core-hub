# ADR-003: Evidence is separate from certification

Date: 2026-10-09. Accepted.

Pinned upstream releases are reproducible, not proven suitable for every title. Profiles carry test status and nullable testedBuild. Baseline profiles are upstream-informed, without local gameplay certification. Enhancements default conservatively; no unverified frame-rate patch enables itself.

Product IDs establish recognition, not pristine-dump verification. Unknown and modified images remain usable with visible status. Hash records require provenance and revision/representation identity. Synthetic fixtures validate mechanisms without commercial content.

PS3/360 editions remain independent. Old regressions, closed issues and Linux-only observations do not automatically determine Windows defaults. Preserve uncertainty rather than inventing a permanent winner.

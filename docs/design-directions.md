# Library direction study
Date: 2026-10-09. Impeccable init → shape → new-work. Mode: Operate.

## Workflow requirements
First run has no media, so imported state must not be faked. Scan/import is the primary empty-library action; Play takes over only after prerequisites resolve. A collection mixes platforms and regions. Edition selection is adjacent to title details. Setup and failed launch must explain the next action without asking the user to understand CPU decoder names.

## Grounded candidates
1. Physical collection shelf: strong recognition, artwork-led browsing; art rights are a dependency.
2. Japanese technical publication index: precise hierarchy and compact labels; avoid editorial page cosplay.
3. Model-kit instruction booklet: sequential setup clarity; unsuitable as permanent navigation.
4. Disc case spine collection: dense generation grouping; narrow titles could become illegible.
5. Printed equipment inventory: excellent install-state comparison; risks feeling like administration.
6. Archive accession folio: title rows and a persistent edition/detail panel; strong collection clarity without needing licensed art.
7. Racing entry list: clear variants and setup states; weak historical connection to much of the catalog.

The Impeccable direction roll assigned candidate 6. The brief's restrained industrial tone constrains it: mineral charcoal, warm white, muted bronze, precise horizontal separators; standard native controls. Signature: each title has a compact generation/catalog mark, and the selected edition's readiness checklist sits next to Play.

## Challengers evaluated
Axes: audience identification and product clarity.
- Design annual plate section: competitive on clarity, weaker collection identity. Becomes a light Edition Desk alternative. Keep its disciplined alignment.
- Labanotation: declined on both axes. Keep simultaneous prerequisite visibility, not symbolic notation.
- Convention circle catalog: competitive on collection identity, less legible readiness. Becomes Collection Shelf alternative.
- Airline ticket wallet: declined on both axes. Keep an explicit step-by-step setup progression.
- Centre-rail reference setting: declined on identity, competitive on density; small columns conflict with title lengths. Keep clear supporting notes.
- Split-flap concourse: declined on identity, competitive on status scanability. Keep stable status columns without animated flaps.

## Three working prototypes
`outputs/Design-review.html` contains interactive synthetic examples, not the application or compatibility evidence.
- **Archive**: dense title list and persistent details, clear missing setup, most robust without cover art. Default for unattended implementation. Risk: requires careful type/selection polish to avoid looking austere.
- **Collection Shelf**: large original typographic covers, top navigation, selected title expands below. Strong library identity; slower comparison of missing prerequisites and many editions.
- **Edition Desk**: light table with side detail, explicit platform/region/build columns. Strong advanced comparison; less comfortable in a dark gaming room and more information than a new user needs.

User review is invited before final design acceptance. Implementation may proceed with the reversible Archive default while unattended; the user did not approve a final direction yet.

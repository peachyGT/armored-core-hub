# What we've tested

We've checked launches and sampled gameplay with our autonomous harnesses. These checks cover the games we tried on our test machines; they do not certify every region, console version, control profile or full playthrough.

The maintainer clarified on 2026-10-14 that Xbox 360 testing is limited to Armored Core V and Verdict Day. The Xbox 360 versions of Armored Core 4 and For Answer have not been tested. The detailed published Xenia record is for V; this clarification does not add measured Verdict Day results or whole-game certification.

The [gameplay report](2026-10-10/RESULTS.md) records editions, sampled controls, graphics, saves and problems found. It includes AC4 and For Answer on PS3, with an older RPCS3 build used after a newer build stalled. Stable framerates in those sampled runs are not a promise for every PC, mission or emulator build.

Application tests are separate. The default suite currently has 205 checks; optional BIOS, SDL and MP4 fixtures brought the [0.3.10 audit](2026-10-13-playtime-shutdown/RESULTS.md) to 209. Fresh and relocated portable copies each passed 50 native app checks. These test the Hub's behavior, not completion of the games.

The Hub imports your local game files and automatically downloads and configures the supported emulators. It does not download commercial games. AC VI and Windows Mobile releases are outside the current catalog. Some formats, achievement sets and community bindings have specific limits; see the [feature matrix](../feature-matrix.md) and [known limits](../../BLOCKED.md).
